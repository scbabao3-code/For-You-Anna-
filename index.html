A letter just for you

Hey, Anna. Take a breath with me.

I know the last little while has been a lot to carry. The meeting at school, the investigation, the tension between families, and hearing that an allegation was being made about you — especially something you say isn't true — must have felt shocking and deeply unfair. I can understand why you might feel angry, confused, hurt, or betrayed all at once.

You don't have to force yourself to feel only one thing. Sometimes your heart can miss someone and feel hurt by them at the same time. I know you loved Divan. Those feelings were real, and you don't have to feel embarrassed about having cared for him. Loving someone doesn't make you foolish. It means you let yourself care.

And if you're in shock right now, you don't need to make every decision today. You don't have to decide what you feel about him forever, forgive anyone before you're ready, or explain your side to every person who has an opinion. Keep telling the truth as you know it, write down what you remember, and let the trusted adults involved handle the investigation fairly. You deserve to be heard without having to carry the whole thing on your own.

You can love someone and still choose distance. You can be hurt and still be kind to yourself. You can take your time.

Big sister, big brother, best friend — whatever you need from the people who love you, you deserve patience and support while you work through this. Please lean on your mum, your trusted teacher, or another safe adult when things feel too heavy. You shouldn't have to hold it all in by yourself.

And Anna, I need you to remember that this situation is one chapter of your life. It is not your whole story. It doesn't erase your good qualities, your future, your humour, your hopes, or the parts of you that make people care about you.

I'm in your corner. One day, one breath, one step at a time.

🌷

You are allowed to be human.

Your mistakes, awkward moments, strong feelings, and difficult days are part of being human. You can learn from things without turning every mistake into a reason to be cruel to yourself. You do not have to be perfect to be loved and respected.

✨

Your heart is not a weakness.

The fact that you cared deeply does not make you weak or silly. Your loyalty and your ability to open up matter. Keep that kind heart — just let trust grow alongside consistent actions, honesty, and respect.

⭐

You're a star, Anna.

You don't have to chase every fish in the sea to prove you're worth choosing. If someone's actions leave you feeling small or unsafe in a relationship, you can step back and protect your peace. Your worth isn't decided by who stays, who leaves, or what anyone says about you.

🫶

What makes you “you” is worth keeping.

Your voice, your feelings, your hopes, your courage to tell your side, and all the little things that make you Anna deserve room to exist. Growth doesn't mean becoming someone else. It means learning to care for yourself while staying true to who you are.

For the moment everything feels too much

Put both feet on the floor. Let your shoulders drop. Take one slow breath, then another. You don't have to solve your whole life in this moment. Find someone safe, tell them honestly how you're feeling, and let yourself be supported. Just get through this moment gently; the next step can come later.
